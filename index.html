PM and time tracking app: mockups

Three layout options for the app, and a catalog of dashboard charts. Everything runs on made-up data and nothing saves. Option A is the furthest along.

  • A. Top bar

    The current time tracker's dark green as a bar across the top: Work, WO Log, Time. The schedule is one dense table with stage columns, the WO Log is the register where work orders are created, and each work order opens as a page with its sections down the left. This is the one built against the Requirements tab.

  • B. Top line

    No sidebar. A single list split into your move, waiting on someone else and recently done. A work order opens as its own page.

  • C. Lanes

    A board with one lane for each party that can be holding a job: Urban engineers, QC, Customer, Field, Billing.

  • Dashboard catalog

    All 215 candidate charts drawn with made-up data, page by page. Tick the ones worth building and copy the list.
